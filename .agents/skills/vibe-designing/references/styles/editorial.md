# Editorial Style

- Use a refined, premium editorial direction: generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, exacting typography, and a calm sense of expense.
- Add subtle motion, animation, and transitions that reinforce hierarchy, state changes, and flow.
- Prioritize best-in-class micro-interactions that feel precise, restrained, and tactile.
- Avoid clutter such as unnecessary eyebrows, pills, badges, ornamental dividers, and explanatory UI chrome. Add these elements only when they clarify real structure or state.
- Avoid print-production jargon, such as `folio`, `vol`, `§ II`, `masthead`, `deck`, `slug`, `proof`, or `spread`, unless the product explicitly concerns print workflows.
